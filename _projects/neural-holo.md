Neural-Holo turns live camera input into depth maps and holographic phase patterns for projection through a spatial light modulator.

We built it for the UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026, combining computer vision, PyTorch, Optuna, and a physical optics setup with a camera, laser, and 4f system.
